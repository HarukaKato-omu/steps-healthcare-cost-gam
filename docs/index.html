How to read this

  • An observed association in one selected cohort (long-term A-Smile users aged 40+ in Osaka, 2020–2023), not the effect of changing a person's steps.
  • A back-transformed log-model prediction (geometric-mean type), far below the arithmetic mean; not for budget totals. The 95% CI is point-wise for the curve, not for an individual.
  • Ages 40–74; other covariates fixed at the sample median/mode. Steps outside 845–14,906/day are extrapolated.
